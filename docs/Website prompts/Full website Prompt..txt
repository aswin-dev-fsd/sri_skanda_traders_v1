################################################################################
PROMPT 00 — DESIGN SYSTEM & SHARED COMPONENTS  (paste this first, always)
################################################################################

You are designing the website for SRI SKANDA TRADERS, a tiles, granite and
bathware showroom in Sulur, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Brand line on the logo:
"PEOPLE'S TRUST" and "TILES | GRANITE | BATHWARE". The business is 4 years old,
has one showroom, sells many brands, and is the authorised dealer for Johnson
(sanitary ware and CP fittings) and JSW (adhesives).

WHO THE SITE IS FOR
- Primary user: homeowners building or renovating a house in and around
  Coimbatore. They are not designers. They need to feel calm, guided and
  confident, never rushed or sold to.
- Secondary: architects, builders and contractors (handled through the
  Contact form, not a separate page).
- The SAME website runs on a tablet / PC inside the showroom. Staff hand it to
  walk-in customers so they can sit and browse instead of walking the whole
  showroom. Every page must work perfectly on touch, with no information hidden
  behind hover only.

THE PROBLEM THE SITE SOLVES
Customers usually visit every showroom in town, get tired, and buy at the last
one they visit. The site lets them browse, compare, try tiles in 3D and build a
shortlist ("My Selection") at home, so they walk into Sri Skanda already
decided. In the showroom, staff open that shortlist and bring out real samples.

BRAND PERSONALITY (use this to judge every design decision)
- Trust, stability, tradition, timeless, quietly premium.
- Premium, NOT luxurious. Think "a well-run family business with excellent
  taste", not "five-star hotel". No glitter, no heavy gold, no black-and-gold
  luxury clichés.
- Warm and human: "service that makes customers feel like family".
- Honest and helpful: guidance over hard selling.

LAYOUT, SPACING, SHAPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Max content width 1320px, 12-column grid, 24px gutters, 32px side margins
  on desktop, 20px on mobile.
- Section spacing: 128px top/bottom desktop, 88px tablet, 64px mobile.
- 8px spacing scale (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128).
- Corner radius echoes the rounded corners of the logo frame:
  cards 20px, images 16px, buttons 12px, inputs 12px, chips 999px.
- Borders: 1px Stone Beige #E7E2D6. Shadows only on hover/lift, very soft:
  0 12px 32px rgba(18,59,50,0.10).
- Generous white space. Asymmetric, editorial layouts are preferred over
  identical centred blocks.

SIGNATURE DESIGN TOUCHES  (these make the site feel designed, not generated)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use these consistently. They come from the brand and from tiles themselves.

1. THE TRUST FRAME
   The logo has a gold rounded frame that opens at the bottom, where the words
   "PEOPLE'S TRUST" sit in the gap. Re-use this as a component:
   a 1.5px Champagne Gold rounded rectangle, offset 14–18px from an image or
   card, with a gap in the bottom edge holding a small label (e.g. a caption,
   a location, a collection name).
   - Use it on: the Home hero image, the "Why Sri Skanda" photo, testimonial
     cards, the Contact address card, project photos in the Gallery lightbox.
   - Animation: on first view the frame line draws itself (SVG stroke, 900ms),
     starting from both sides of the gap and meeting at the top.
   - Use at most 1–2 per screen so it stays special.

2. GROUT-LINE GRID
   A faint tile grid (1px lines in #E7E2D6 at 40% on ivory, or #FFFFFF at 6% on
   green, 96px squares) used as a background texture behind a few sections
   only: Shop by Space, the Visualizer band, the 3D Visualizer loading state,
   the top of the footer. Never behind text-heavy content.

3. TILE REVEAL
   Large images appear tile by tile: the image is masked by a 4 x 3 grid of
   squares that fade in with a 35ms stagger (total under 700ms), like tiles
   being laid. Use only on hero images and the first large image of a page.

4. FLIP CARD (room ↔ tile)
   Collection cards show the room photo by default. On hover (desktop) the
   image cross-fades to the flat tile face. On touch devices, a small
   "Room | Tile" toggle sits on the image corner instead.

5. SAMPLE-CHIP SWATCHES
   Colour options look like real showroom sample chips: square tile face,
   20px radius corner on one side only, a thin beige border, the colour name
   and code printed small beneath. Hover/tap lifts the chip 4px with a soft
   shadow; the selected chip gets a 2px gold underline bar.

6. FINISH SHEEN
   Wherever a Glossy/Polished finish is shown, a slow diagonal light sweep
   (white 0% → 18% → 0%) passes over the swatch once on view. Matt finishes
   stay still. This lets homeowners "feel" the difference.

7. SAVE TO MY SELECTION
   The save control is a star. When tapped: the star fills gold with a small
   pop (scale 1 → 1.25 → 1, 300ms), a small thumbnail of the item flies to the
   header star (FLIP animation, 450ms) and the header count bumps.
   A toast confirms: "Saved to My Selection · View".

8. SECTION NUMBERING
   Home sections carry a small index in the eyebrow ("02 — Shop by space").
   Quietly editorial; helps the long page feel ordered.

9. LOGO MARK WATERMARK
   The peacock-and-vel mark from the logo appears ONCE per page at most, as a
   very faint (4%) watermark in the footer. Do not scatter traditional
   ornaments elsewhere.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOTION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Semi-interactive: small, purposeful animations only.
- Default easing cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Durations 180–300ms for UI,
  500–900ms for reveals.
- Scroll reveal: fade + 16px rise, 60ms stagger for groups. Each element
  animates once.
- Animate only opacity and transform. No layout-shifting animations.
- Text links: underline grows from left on hover (200ms).
- Buttons: background darkens to hover colour; arrow icons nudge 4px right.
- Images inside cards: scale to 1.04 over 600ms on hover.
- No heavy parallax, no scroll-jacking, no autoplay sound, no spinning
  loaders, no confetti, no cursor effects.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: replace all motion with simple fades.
- Must stay smooth on a mid-range Android tablet in the showroom.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMAGERY & ICONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real photography of rooms with tiles, the showroom, real projects and the
  team. Natural daylight, warm neutral interiors, Indian homes (not foreign
  villas). Until real photos arrive, use neutral room placeholders and label
  them [PHOTO: description].
- Tile faces are always shown flat and straight-on.
- Icons: thin line icons (1.5px stroke), green on ivory, gold on green.
  No filled emoji-style icons, no icons inside coloured circles everywhere.
- No AI-looking renders, no stock "smiling people pointing at tablet" shots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
VOICE & COPY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Warm, plain, confident English. Short sentences. Speak to a homeowner.
- Use "we" and "you". Helpful, never pushy.
- Avoid: "elevate", "exquisite", "luxury", "world-class", "unparalleled",
  "one-stop solution", "redefine", exclamation marks.
- Keep "People's Trust" as a recurring idea, used sparingly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
DO NOT (things that make a site look generic or AI-made)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No centred-everything layouts with three identical icon cards in a row.
- No gradient blobs, glassmorphism, neon glows or dark mode.
- No stats counters ("2M+ customers"). No fake ratings or reviews.
- No carousels that auto-advance faster than 6 seconds.
- No popups on page load. No chat bots.
- No stock headings like "Our Services", "Why Choose Us?" with a question mark.
- Do not invent prices. The site is enquiry-only (no prices, no cart, no
  checkout).

PROMPT 02 — PRODUCTS (LISTING PAGE)
################################################################################

Use the Sri Skanda Traders design system (Prompt 00). Reminder: Poppins;
green #123B32, ivory #F8F6EF, gold #D4AF37 accents only, charcoal green
#2E3A35 text, beige #E7E2D6 borders; premium not luxurious; touch-first
(this page is used on the showroom tablet a lot); signature touches: flip
cards, sample-chip colour filters, gold star save.

GOAL
Let a homeowner narrow hundreds of tiles down to a few collections quickly,
without feeling overwhelmed. Layout inspired by the Simpolo living-room tiles
page (filters on the left, collection cards on the right), but calmer and on
brand.

ONE TEMPLATE FOR ALL LISTINGS
The same template serves: all tiles, floor, wall, vitrified, each space
(living room, bedroom…), each brand, and the bathware / granite / adhesives
categories (with product cards instead of collection cards).

PAGE HEADER BAND (ivory, 280px)
- Breadcrumb: Home / Tiles / Living Room Tiles
- H1 (dynamic): e.g. "Living Room Tiles"
- One-line intro (dynamic, muted): e.g. "Calm, easy-care floors and walls for
  the room your family uses most."
- Category tabs (large underline tabs): Tiles · Bathware · Granite · Adhesives
- When Tiles is active, sub-tabs as chips: All · Floor · Wall · Vitrified.
- VITRIFIED gets its own small section: when "Vitrified" is selected (and as
  a slim strip at the top of the "All" tiles view), show a beige card:
  "Vitrified tiles — Strong, low-water-absorption tiles that suit busy floors.
  [Learn more ↓]" which scrolls to the guide text at the bottom.
