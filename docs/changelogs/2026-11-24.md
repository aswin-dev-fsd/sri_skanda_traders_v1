# 2026-11-24

## Change: 3D visualizer restyle, Advanced settings, phone tabs

- **Added:** backups of the visualizer from before this change: `docs/backups/visualizer-2026-11-24.html` and `docs/backups/VisualizerEmbed-2026-11-24.astro`, plus git tag `backup/visualizer-2026-11-24` (local, not pushed). Listed in `docs/README.md`.
- **Changed:** the visualizer now has the site's look: ivory panels, green selected and primary controls, gold only as an accent, Poppins at 14px, larger touch targets (40–44px), and our logo instead of the "360°" badge (`public/embed/visualizer.html`). Added `public/embed/fonts/` (Poppins 400–700, copied from `@fontsource/poppins`) and `public/embed/logo-mark.png`.
- **Changed:** the technical controls are kept but out of the way. A new **Advanced settings** panel (top bar) holds Show (Real look · Bumps · Shine · Plain), Picture quality, Zoom, Brightness, "Show direction and zoom on screen" and the Tile list. Edge depth and Shine are folded under **More settings** in "Adjust the look". The direction and zoom readout is off by default.
- **Changed:** layout. Below 900px the panels become one bottom sheet with tabs Surface · Tile · Adjust · Room, closed by default; Light, Taps & fittings and Stand at move into Room. On tablet the top bar drops its group labels and button words so it fits (it was clipped). Panels fade aside while you drag to look around and return when you let go. "Turn the tiles" and "Finish" are now separate rows.
- **Fixed:** on phones the site's floating WhatsApp button covered the visualizer's Enquire button. It now hides while the 3D view is on screen (`src/components/layout/WhatsAppButton.astro`, `src/components/visualizer/VisualizerEmbed.astro`); `tests/e2e/visualizer.spec.ts` checks it.
- **Removed:** the gold "DEMO" chip. "Sample bathroom and sample tiles" stays under the name on wider screens.
- **Checked:** `npm run build` passes; visualizer, accessibility and overflow tests pass. The real visualizer was driven in Chromium at 1440, 1024 and 360 wide: nothing overflows, Poppins loads, no control text under 13px, Advanced settings and every phone tab open, and the floating WhatsApp button hides. The drag-to-hide panels were checked with the room's rendering paused, because the test browser's software 3D draws only about one frame every two seconds.
- **Decisions:** technical controls are kept in Advanced settings and More settings rather than removed (user's choice); the DEMO chip is removed; phone layout is a tabbed bottom sheet; the 3-step flow and the speed work are future plans. Recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` → 3D Visualizer page.
- **Open:**
  - The guided 3-step flow (3) and a faster visualizer (9) are recorded as future plans in `docs/DECISIONS.md` → 3D Visualizer page; neither is started.
  - On the 1024 tablet the two side panels leave about 350px of room in the middle; the 3-step flow plan addresses this.
  - The tiles are still the demo's made-up tiles, now without the DEMO chip.
  - Test on the real showroom tablet and a real phone.
