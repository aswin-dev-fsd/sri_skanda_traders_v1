# 2026-11-23

## Change: 3D visualizer in plain words, with our actions at the bottom

- **Changed:** every label, toast and the tile list in the demo visualizer now uses everyday words instead of 3D jargon. Examples: "Curated Hero Tiles" → "Choose a tile", "Surface PBR Inspector" → "Adjust the look", "Grout Joint Width" → "Gap between tiles", "Inside Standing Spot" → "Stand at", lighting → "Day · Evening · Night". Surfaces are named by what they are ("Main floor", "Basin wall", "Shower shelf"), finishes read Glossy · Satin · Matt · Textured, and sizes read "600 × 1200 mm" (`public/embed/visualizer.html`).
- **Added:** a bottom action bar in the visualizer. Left: Undo, Redo, Start again (moved from the top bar and right panel). Right: Save picture (was "Snapshot"), ☆ Save to My Selection, WhatsApp and Enquire. Buttons are 44px tall. On phones the room actions show icons only, Redo is hidden, and below 440px Save picture is hidden so the bar fits. The camera bar and side panels moved up to make room. "Hide controls" hides the new bar too (`public/embed/visualizer.html`).
- **Added:** the visualizer page answers the embed's buttons. Save adds or removes the tile in view (name, code, size, finish, small thumbnail) in My Selection with the usual toast. Enquire opens the enquiry drawer about that tile, leaving full screen first. WhatsApp opens a chat that names the tile and size. In full screen, where the site toast is hidden, the embed shows the message itself. The embed shows these three buttons only when the page answers, so opened on its own they stay hidden (`src/components/visualizer/VisualizerEmbed.astro`).
- **Added:** a test in `tests/e2e/visualizer.spec.ts` that a stub embed's Save reaches My Selection and its Enquire opens the drawer about the tile; listed in `docs/TESTING.md`.
- **Checked:** `npm run build` passes; visualizer, accessibility and phone-overflow tests pass; the real visualizer was driven in Chromium at 1440, 1024 and 360 wide (save, toast, enquiry drawer, WhatsApp link, no bar overflow).
- **Decisions:** the visualizer is no longer limited to branding edits: labels and the action bar changed, and it can now save to My Selection. The future UI plan (restyle in site colours, remove technical controls, 3-step flow, touch-first layout) is recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` → 3D Visualizer page.
- **Open:**
  - The visualizer's top bar is still clipped on tablet and phone (the Show buttons are cut off at 1024). It is part of the future UI plan.
  - On phones, the site's floating WhatsApp button sits over the visualizer's bottom-right corner (the Enquire button) when the frame fills the screen.
  - Saved visualizer tiles are the demo's made-up tiles; they link back to `/visualizer` and cannot reopen that tile.
  - Test on the real showroom tablet.
