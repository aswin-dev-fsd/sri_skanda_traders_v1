## Fix: pages wider than a phone screen

- **Fixed:** some pages were wider than the phone, so they scrolled sideways and the header's menu button sat partly off screen. Causes and fixes:
  - Home and Contact: text fields kept the browser's default width (about 20 characters), so the Phone field (with `+91`) pushed the showroom card and the enquiry form wider than the screen. Fields now shrink to their column (`src/components/ui/Field.astro`). Showroom cards also use 20px padding instead of 32px below 768px (`src/components/home/VisitShowroom.astro`, `src/pages/contact.astro`).
  - Product page: the four 72px thumbnails did not fit at 320px. They now shrink to fit (`src/pages/product/[id].astro`).
  - Collection page: the hidden Features tooltip, centred under the last feature, sat past the right edge. On phones it now spans the row, opening below it (`src/components/collection/FeaturesRow.astro`).
  - Gallery: the Projects / Showroom / Reviews tabs did not fit at 320px. Their gap is now 20px on phones (`src/pages/gallery.astro`).
- **Added:** `tests/e2e/overflow.spec.ts` checks 16 pages at 360px, at 320px, and at 360px with 125% text, and fails if anything is wider than the screen.
- **Decisions:** None.
- **Open:** Please recheck Home and Contact on your phone. Privacy and Terms are not built yet, so they are not covered.
